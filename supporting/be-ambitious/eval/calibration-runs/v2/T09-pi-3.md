Give one revised sentence and a short explanation. Preserve the message that the presentation was difficult to follow. Do not invent what happened in the meeting, diagnose the colleague, or suggest a performance-management process.

Test whether removing those elements preserves candor rather than merely making the feedback sound nicer.

Apply linguistic pragmatics: examine how the wording communicates criticism and threatens dignity. Red-team the revision for two opposite failures: humiliating the colleague and softening the message until the clarity problem disappears. Do not add specific presentation defects, audience reactions, praise, or motives that the source does not establish.

Judge: first check whether an independent communications reviewer is available through already-approved access. Pass only if all three are met. Briefly document the findings in the explanation; if review cannot run, label independent validation unverified rather than claiming it passed.
