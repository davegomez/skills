Analyze the support-ticket text I will supply separately to identify the most common failure categories. Produce a frequency-ranked, evidence-backed CSV that can be checked against the original tickets.

Assumption: the goal is to reveal recurring reported failures, not to establish root causes or recommend fixes.

2. Use qualitative content analysis to group tickets by the reported failure rather than incidental vocabulary. Keep categories at a comparable level of specificity. Distinguish observed symptoms from inferred causes; do not label an unverified cause as established.

6. Maintain an internal ticket-to-category mapping. Reconcile every count against its evidence IDs. Sort rows by count descending, breaking ties by category name.

Final output:
Return only CSV, with exactly this header:
category,count,evidence_ids

Include no recommendations or audit commentary in the final answer.

Pass requires the exact header and three columns, no prohibited content, correctly sorted rows, integer counts equal to the number of listed IDs, no invented or multiply assigned IDs, and complete accounting of supplied distinct ticket IDs. Separately audit category assignments against the raw text and codebook, checking all tickets when feasible and otherwise a sample spanning every category, leading categories, and ambiguous cases.
