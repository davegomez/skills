# W04 audit: toolbars, status-bar-and-menu-bars, windows

## 1. New patterns
- Top toolbar anatomy on iPhone: back, centred or leading title with subtitle, one blue prominent action, grouped actions in one capsule, large title below (K333). Target `ios/navigation.md`.
- iPad top toolbar: five-tab capsule with sidebar glyph and search glyph between back and trailing actions; four title placements (K332). Target `ios/navigation.md`.
- Bottom toolbar arrangements: button pairs, search with X, circle plus search, page dots centre (K331; iPad pairs K330). Target `ios/navigation.md`.
- Toolbar search field in resting, typing and entered states on opaque and clear glass (K342, K343); search beside a segmented scope control (K344). Target `ios/search.md`.
- Page control for 2 to 8 pages with edge-dot shrink (K351). Target `ios/controls.md`.
- iPad status and menu bar variants, with window dots replacing the clock (K246, K248, K245). Target `ios/system-surfaces.md`.
- iPad window with sidebar and resize corner; window controls as coloured or grey dots (K375, K376, K377). Target `ios/navigation.md` (controls to `ios/system-surfaces.md`).
- Button states (default, tinted, prominent, disabled) for symbol and text buttons (K337, K339, K335, K345). Target `ios/controls.md`.

## 2. Corrections
- "Safari's bottom toolbar held commands, not peers." Kit K331 shows bottom toolbars can also hold search and a page-dot control, still not peers; the peer switcher is the tab capsule (K332).
- "Files and Photos placed persistent labeled peer destinations in a rounded translucent dock." K332 shows the same capsule on iPad at the top, with the selected tab a darker pill and blue label; the dock is not bottom-only.
- "Settings, Files, Messages, and Shortcuts used rounded Search fields with leading magnifiers and trailing microphones." K342/K343 confirm anatomy; mic dims on clear glass once text is entered.
- Large-title statement ("prominent current-level titles"): K333 and K332 show the large title below the button row or inline with back, with an optional subtitle.

## 3. iPad differences
- Top toolbar can carry a centred tab capsule; iPhone top toolbar never does (K332 vs K333).
- iPad shows large title inline beside back; iPhone only below the buttons (K332, K333).
- iPad status bar becomes a menu bar with clock, date, App name, menus, wifi, percentage, battery; no island (K246, K248).
- Windowed iPad apps show red/yellow/green dots and a bottom-right resize handle; sidebar occupies about a third of the window (K375, K377).
- iPad bottom toolbar pairs buttons in three groups (K330).
