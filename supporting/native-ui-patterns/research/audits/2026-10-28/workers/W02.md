## 3. iPad differences
- Alerts, action sheets, share, color picker and menus are anchored popovers or small centered cards, not bottom sheets (K055, K056, K057, K058, K059).
- Sheets are floating inset cards, not full-width (K070); the keyboard sits below it.
- Tab bar is a top floating capsule with sidebar toggle (K072); sidebar is a translucent left column with selected-row capsule and collapsible section headings (K071).
- Lists are centered, readable-width inset cards on gray with the title at the left and grouped trailing buttons (K065).
- Toolbars: Search field in the top bar, large and compact large titles, bottom toolbar clusters (K073).
- Windows with traffic-light controls and resize grip (K074); Control Center tiles appear on the Lock Screen right (K060).
- Widgets reflow icons in a 6-column grid (K062, K063); notifications are a centered narrow column (K068).
