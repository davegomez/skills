## Comparison boundary

The new no-results comparison row is limited to the directly supported shared UX problem in each platform's Settings app. Platform-only pre-commit and silent-input behaviors were not added to the cross-platform table. Shared rows in [observed patterns](../docs/comparison/observed-patterns.md) keep each platform's presentation and evidence separate; similar purpose does not confirm equivalent visuals, semantics, interaction, or implementation.
