# iOS controls

## Guidance

**Observed:** Settings used trailing switches in on and off states with gray footer captions, an inline slider with bounding glyphs, and preview-tile pickers with a check or capsule selection cue. **Kit:** buttons come filled, tinted, or plain in 3 sizes, blue or red for destructive, each with a gray disabled twin; glass buttons pair a saturated prominent style with a white neutral one. Switches show a green on track and a gray off track; sliders add tick dots. Pressed switches and sliders show an enlarged glass knob, and disabled ones turn pale. A segmented control marks its selection with a raised white pill. **Inferred adaptation:** match the control to the value type, keep one prominent button per area, and pair color with a second state cue. Drag behavior, haptics, and spoken values remain **Unconfirmed**.

## Review checks

Check applicable on/off, pressed, disabled, selected, minimum and maximum, determinate and indeterminate, and placeholder, focused, and filled states. Give each state a cue beyond color.
