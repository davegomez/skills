# iOS system surfaces

## Guidance

**Kit:** Home Screen widgets come in 4 sizes, each with a dashed content margin; iPad adds an extra-large wide size. The Lock Screen stacks a date line, the time, and a widget row, with flashlight and camera buttons in the bottom corners; Lock Screen widgets are inline text or circular. Notifications stack as glass banners; the expanded form is a card with custom content and a separate action list. Control Center mixes 1×1 to 2×2 tiles with tall sliders. Home Screen quick actions list system rows first, Remove App in red, then app actions and a widget-size row. On iPad a menu bar replaces the status bar, with clock, date, bold app name, and optional menus. **Inferred adaptation:** design widget and control content per size slot, keep notification and quick-action labels short, and give app icons artwork that reads on light, dark, and tinted grounds. Point sizes, refresh, interaction, and system behavior remain **Unconfirmed**; verify them in current Apple documentation.
